Delivered Fusion Agentic Applications¶

Oracle delivers prebuilt Fusion Agentic Applications alongside the apps you build yourself. This catalog covers Releases 26B, 26C and 26D

What they have in common¶

Characteristic Description
Outcome-driven coordination Teams of agents deliver complete business results, not isolated tasks
Adaptive user experience The UX adapts to the user and the work at hand
Autonomous actions Routine actions run on their own; exceptions and judgment calls go to people
Native Fusion foundation Built on the Fusion data model, security, business rules, approvals and audit

Extending delivered apps¶

You can use the Agentic Application Builder to extend delivered Agentic Applications or build new apps around processes that need a particular combination of agents. Agents can come from Oracle, partners or your own team, and the Oracle AI Agent Marketplace also offers reusable agents, workflows, connectors, templates and agentic applications.
